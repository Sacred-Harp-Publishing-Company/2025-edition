# HOLBROOK

When the storms of life are rag·ing,
Stand by me, oh, stand by me.
In the midst of trib·u·la·tion,
Stand by me, oh, stand by me.
When the world is toss·ing me
Like a ship up·on the sea,
Thou who rul·est wind and wa·ter,
Stand by me, oh, stand by me.

In the midst of faults and fail·ures,
Stand by me, oh, stand by me.
In the midst of per·se·cu·tion,
Stand by me, oh, stand by me.
When I do the best I can,
And my friends mis·un·der·stand,
Thou who know·est all a·bout me,
Stand by me, oh, stand by me.
