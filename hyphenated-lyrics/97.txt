# WE’LL SOON BE THERE

Oh, who will come and go with me?
We’ll shout and sing Ho·san·na;
I’m bound fair Ca·naan’s land to see,
We’ll shout and sing Ho·san·na.

[chorus]
Go on, go on, we’ll soon be there,
We’ll shout and sing Ho·san·na;
Come on, come on, we’ll soon be there,
We’ll shout and sing Ho·san·na.
