# HAMPTON

Ye flow·’ry plains, pro·claim His skill;
Val·leys lie low be·fore His eye;
And let His praise from ev·’ry hill
Rise tune·ful to the neigh·b’ring sky.

Speak of the won·ders of that love
Which Ga·briel plays on ev·’ry chord:
From all be·low, and all a·bove,
Loud hal·le·lu·jahs to the Lord.
