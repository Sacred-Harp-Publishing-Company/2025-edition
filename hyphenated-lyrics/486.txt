# BENEFICENCE

That man is blest who stands in awe
Of God, and loves His sa·cred law:
His seed on earth shall be re·nowned
And with suc·ces·sive hon·ors crowned.
His house a seat of wealth shall be,
An un·ex·haust·ed treas·ur·y;
His jus·tice, free from all de·cay,
Shall bless·ings to his heirs con·vey.
