# DAY OF WORSHIP

Dear peo·ple, we have met to·day
To sing, to hear, to preach and pray;
It is our Fa·ther’s great com·mand,
The road that leads to His right hand;
But oh, the sad and aw·ful state
Of those who stand and come too late:
The fool·ish vir·gins did be·gin
To knock, but could not en·ter in.
