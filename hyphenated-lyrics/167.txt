# PRAY, BRETHREN, PRAY

Pray, breth·ren, pray, the prayer of faith a·vail·eth;
Pray, breth·ren, pray, on God your spir·it stay.

[chorus]
A few more days of sor·row, and the Lord will call us home,
To walk the gold·en streets of the new Je·ru·sa·lem.

Sing, breth·ren, sing, re·joice with joy and glad·ness;
Sing, breth·ren, sing, your songs of tri·umph bring.

Rise, breth·ren, rise, your souls by faith as·cend·ing;
Rise, breth·ren, rise, your home is in the skies.
