# BREMEN

My God, I am Thine;
What a com·fort di·vine,
What a bless·ing to know that my Je·sus is mine!
In the heav·en·ly Lamb
Thrice hap·py I am,
And my heart it doth dance at the sound of His name.

True pleas·ures a·bound
In the rap·tur·ous sound,
And who·ev·er hath found it hath par·a·dise found:
My Je·sus to know,
And feel His blood flow,
’Tis life ev·er·last·ing, ’tis heav·en be·low.

Yet on·ward I haste
To the heav·en·ly feast:
Oh, that is the full·ness; but this is the taste!
And this I shall prove,
Till with joy I re·move
To the heav·en of heav·ens in Je·sus·’s love.
