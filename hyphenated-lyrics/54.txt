# RUSSELL

Ye hum·ble souls, com·plain no more;
Let faith sur·vey your fu·ture store;
How hap·py, how di·vine·ly blest,
The sa·cred words of truth at·test!
When con·scious grief la·ments sin·cere,
And pours the pen·i·ten·tial tear,
Hope points to your de·ject·ed eyes
The bright re·ver·sion in the skies.
