# SHAWMUT

O that I could re·pent!
With all my i·dols part,
And to Thy gra·cious eye pre·sent
A hum·ble, con·trite heart.

A heart with grief op·pressed
For hav·ing grieved my God,
A trou·bled heart that can·not rest
Till sprin·kled with Thy blood.

Je·sus, on me be·stow
The pen·i·tent de·sire;
With true sin·cer·i·ty of woe
My ach·ing breast in·spire.

With soft·’ning pit·y look,
And melt my hard·ness down;
Strike with Thy love’s re·sist·less stroke,
And break this heart of stone.
