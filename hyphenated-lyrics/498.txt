# THE RESURRECTION DAY

Oh, Res·ur·rec·tion Day!
When Christ the Sav·ior comes some morn·ing fair,
The grave will o·pen wide
And saints will rise to meet Him in the air.

[chorus]
Oh! may we meet in heav’n
To sing the prais·es of our Lord and King,
And gath·ered round the snow-white throne
Make heav·en’s por·tals ring.
