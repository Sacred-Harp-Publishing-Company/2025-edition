# NIGHT SONG

Great trou·bles with tu·mul·tuous noise
Like swell·ing seas a·round me spread;
Thy ris·ing waves drown all my joys,
And roll tre·men·dous o’er my head.
Yet will the Lord pro·vide His love
When I ad·dress His throne by day,
Nor in the night His grace re·move;
The night shall hear me sing and pray.
