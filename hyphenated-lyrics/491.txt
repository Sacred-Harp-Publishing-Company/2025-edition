# EAGLES’ WINGS

O glo·rious hope of per·fect love!
It lifts me up to things a·bove,
It bears on ea·gles’ wings;
It gives my rap·tured soul a taste,
And makes me for some mo·ments feast
With Je·sus’ priests and kings.

No foot of land do I pos·sess,
No cot·tage in the wil·der·ness,
A poor way·far·ing man;
I lodge a·while in tents be·low,
Or glad·ly wan·der as I go,
Till I my Ca·naan gain.
